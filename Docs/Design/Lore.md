# Fuseborn: World & Lore

Living reference for the world. The GDD (§1) summarizes it. Names marked *(placeholder)* are open (GDD §13 #35).

## Premise
In **Alembria** *(placeholder)*, alchemy is everything. Anything can **react** with anything: two things meet, combine, and something new is born. Only the fusion idea is borrowed from chemistry; there are no equations, just alchemy.

The **Great Codex** records every known reaction. Whoever discovers a new reaction first is written into the Codex, and with that comes strength, wealth and fame. The world moves fast. Guilds race to discover, fight over resources, and win more by winning: more resources mean more reactions to try.

## The Player
You lead a newly founded **alchemy guild** *(name chosen by the player)*. You start with a handful of homunculi and a pile of junk. Your goal is to grow into the most famous guild in the Codex by recruiting, reacting and discovering.

**The Alchemist is not always good.** The player experiments to strengthen the guild against Runoffs and rival Alchemists, and controls each homunculus in whatever way works on it: a curse (Wren), harsh "fixing" (Snick), a father's hand (Tobin), trust and responsibility (Ronan), a trade of loot for good food (Silas).

## Tone
**Hopeful, with weight.** Alchemy has a price, and the alchemist pays it. The player is the guild master and the alchemist: every experiment and reaction is the player's own, and so is every risk. Each reaction sends its reagents' souls back to nature; a new homunculus may draw a fresh soul, or none. Souled homunculi have their own will and don't always obey. Humor stays in item flavor, the goblins' antics and some personalities, but not every homunculus's story is light. Tone can vary per homunculus.

## Glossary
| Term | Meaning | Game system |
|---|---|---|
| **Reaction** | Two things combining into a new one; both are used up | Fusion |
| **Homunculus** | A living alchemical being created by a guild. Homunculi take the forms of Alembria's peoples (Elf, Orc, Golem…) and each has its own personality. Any one can become great. | Heroes; tier ≠ power |
| **Principles** (Salt, Mercury, Sulfur) | Everything in Alembria is made of three principles in different proportions: **Salt** (body), **Mercury** (fluidity), **Sulfur** (fire and spirit). A reaction between two reagents rebalances their principles into a new whole, and the new balance can't be predicted from the ingredients. A homunculus's **dominant principle** is set at its creation. Old alchemists knew only Sulfur and Mercury, the pair that reacts; Salt, the body that holds them, came later | Attributes (GDD §4.6.2) |
| **Unstable Reaction** | Every battle is fueled by a reaction between the two guilds' homunculi; the longer it runs, the more unstable it grows: blows hit harder, healing fails | Overtime (GDD §6.12) |
| **Soul** | Drawn from nature by some homunculi at birth (often tied to their race), giving them will and instinct. Soulless ones (e.g. golems, slimes) simply follow. Returns to nature when the homunculus is used in a reaction; the new homunculus does not inherit it. Nature recycles souls: a homunculus may draw a **reincarnated** soul, which keeps only a **temperament** from its past life, never memories (e.g. Grum's curiosity). Nature includes the sky: Elsie's fresh soul arrived as light. There is no heaven or abyss; "heavens" and "abyss" are only names. **Lore only for now**; open to affecting mechanics later (GDD §13) | — (lore only) |
| **Dark being** | An old power that lives inside a hex book in the guild library. It grants dark energy through an **oath**, and asks for absurd payments in return (Grum's: a lot of donuts). What else it wants is an open story hook | Warlock lore (Grum) |
| **Innate trait** | A rare gift some homunculi wake up with (about 1 in 3); most have none. The rarest exist only once in the whole world | Trait roll (GDD §4.6.4) |
| **Reagent** | Any reactable thing: junk, materials, gear, homunculi | Cards |
| **Runoff** | A failed or uncontrolled reaction that leaked into the world as a living creature. Defeating one recovers its reagents. | PvE monsters, card drops |
| **Great Codex** | The world's record of reactions; first discoverers are named forever | Codex, first-discoverer credit |
| **Alchemists' Exchange** | The trade house that supplies guilds and takes surplus reagents | Daily Chest, starter pack, Exchange |
| **Guild rivalry** | Guilds clash over resources; the winner takes the spoils, and the loser keeps its homunculi | Async PvP (no card loss) |
| **Reagent tides** | Each season, the supply of reagents shifts | Seasonal rarity rotation |
| **Coins** | Guild funds | Soft currency |
| **Harvest sites** | Places to gather raw reagents | Mining (later) |

## System Mapping
| System | In-world reason |
|---|---|
| Fusion consumes both cards | A reaction uses up its reagents; their souls return to nature |
| No-match pairs | Some things simply don't react |
| Recipes are surprising | Alchemy defies common sense; that's why discovery is valuable |
| Junk starts every item recipe | Every treasure in Alembria began as junk reacted cleverly |
| Hero levels only in the party | Homunculi grow through real experience |
| Gear socketing can fail | Magic Stones are unstable; a failed bond shatters the stone |
| Recipes are secret, no official list | Discoveries are a guild's fortune; nobody gives them away |
