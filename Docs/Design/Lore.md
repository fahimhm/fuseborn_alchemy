# Fuseborn: World & Lore

Living reference for the world. The GDD (§1) summarizes it. Names marked *(placeholder)* are open (GDD §13 #35).

## Premise
In **Alembria** *(placeholder)*, alchemy is everything. Anything can **react** with anything: two things meet, combine, and something new is born. Only the fusion idea is borrowed from chemistry; there are no equations, just alchemy.

The **Great Codex** records every known reaction. Whoever discovers a new reaction first is written into the Codex, and with that comes strength, wealth and fame. The world moves fast. Guilds race to discover, fight over resources, and win more by winning: more resources mean more reactions to try.

## The Player
You lead a newly founded **alchemy guild** *(name chosen by the player)*. You start with a handful of homunculi and a pile of junk. Your goal is to grow into the most famous guild in the Codex by recruiting, reacting and discovering.

## Tone
**Light adventure.** Hopeful and colorful, with humor in item flavor text (junk becomes treasure) and in the goblins' antics. There are no grim costs: no one dies, and fused homunculi are new beings, not victims.

## Glossary
| Term | Meaning | Game system |
|---|---|---|
| **Reaction** | Two things combining into a new one; both are used up | Fusion |
| **Homunculus** | A living alchemical being created by a guild. Homunculi take the forms of Alembria's peoples (Elf, Orc, Golem…) and each has its own personality. Any one can become great. | Heroes; tier ≠ power |
| **Innate trait** | The random gift a homunculus wakes up with; no two are the same | Unique skill roll |
| **Reagent** | Any reactable thing: junk, materials, gear, homunculi | Cards |
| **Runoff** | A failed or uncontrolled reaction that leaked into the world as a living creature. Defeating one recovers its reagents. | PvE monsters, card drops |
| **Great Codex** | The world's record of reactions; first discoverers are named forever | Codex, first-discoverer credit |
| **Alchemists' Exchange** | The trade house that supplies guilds and takes surplus reagents | Daily Chest, starter pack, Exchange |
| **Guild rivalry** | Guilds clash over resources; the winner takes the spoils, and the loser keeps its homunculi | Async PvP (no card loss) |
| **Reagent tides** | Each season, the supply of reagents shifts | Seasonal rarity rotation |
| **Coins** | Guild funds | Soft currency |
| **Harvest sites** | Places to gather raw reagents | Mining (later) |

## System Mapping
| System | In-world reason |
|---|---|
| Fusion consumes both cards | A reaction uses up its reagents |
| No-match pairs | Some things simply don't react |
| Recipes are surprising | Alchemy defies common sense; that's why discovery is valuable |
| Junk starts every item recipe | Every treasure in Alembria began as junk reacted cleverly |
| Hero levels only in the party | Homunculi grow through real experience |
| Gear socketing can fail | Magic Stones are unstable; a failed bond shatters the stone |
| Recipes are secret, no official list | Discoveries are a guild's fortune; nobody gives them away |
