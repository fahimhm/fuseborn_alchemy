# Items — Fuseborn

**Status:** Draft (Tier I–III approved; Tier IV–V usable items to do). Rules: GDD §4.8 (subtypes), §15.8 (recipe lists; grids in `Docs/Design/Data/ItemGrid*.tsv`). Lore: junk becomes treasure; principles (Lore.md).

## Rules for materials (Tier I–III)
- **Fusion only.** Materials have no battle use and give no XP. Their value is what they become (and selling / the Exchange, GDD §4.3).
- **Principle lean (flavor only):** each material's Codex page names the principle it leans to (Salt / Mercury / Sulfur). No mechanics, and it never hints at recipes. Leans can **flip** from their ingredients (two Stones, Salt, make Flint, Sulfur): the new balance can't be predicted (Lore.md).
- **Junk Codex:** the first discovery of each material fills its Codex page and gives a small one-time reward (GDD §4.8).
- **Art:** each material needs **card art** and an **inventory icon** (the same subject simplified for small size, with a stack-count badge). No equipped look, no battle effect. Humble but charming: real junk with personality. Card frames stay plain (basic rarity is hidden, GDD §4.2.2).
- Tier III descriptions may **hint** at what a material could become (potions, scrolls, charms) but never name a recipe.
